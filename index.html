For our UK guests

Getting from Schiphol to Hoorn

Most guests will likely fly into Amsterdam Schiphol Airport. From there, the train is usually the easiest and best-value option.

Train — our recommendation

Typical journey: around 45 minutes from Schiphol Airport to Hoorn.

Typical fare: around €15 one way.

Schiphol’s railway station is directly beneath the airport terminal, so there is no separate airport transfer needed before you board.

Taxi / Bolt / Uber

Door-to-door is possible, but expect the journey to be considerably more expensive — likely €100+ one way, depending on time and demand.

We’ll also explore whether a minibus or coach makes sense if enough guests arrive on similar flights.
